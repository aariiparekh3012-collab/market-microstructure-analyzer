# Governance

## Project scope

The Real-Time Market Microstructure Analyzer is an independently maintained
research-software project for transparent and reproducible analysis of market
microstructure signals.

## Maintainer

Aarya Parekh is the founding author and current project maintainer.

The maintainer has final authority over:

- the project roadmap;
- acceptance and merging of contributions;
- release timing and version numbers;
- security responses;
- repository settings;
- citation and archival metadata; and
- changes to governance, subject to existing licence rights.

Publishing a fork or submitting a contribution does not grant write,
administrative, release, or endorsement authority over the official repository.

## Decision process

Routine decisions are made through issues and pull requests. Substantial
changes should include:

1. a clear statement of the problem;
2. the proposed design and alternatives considered;
3. effects on compatibility, reproducibility, and research claims;
4. appropriate tests and documentation; and
5. an opportunity for public technical discussion.

The maintainer may accept, request changes to, defer, or decline a proposal.
Reasons should normally be recorded in the relevant issue or pull request.

## Releases

A release should be created only after the relevant automated checks pass.
Release notes should identify meaningful changes, compatibility considerations,
known limitations, and the archived DOI when available.

## Community roles

Contributors are acknowledged through Git history and release notes where
appropriate. Repeated contributors may be invited to help triage issues or
review pull requests, but expanded permissions are granted explicitly and may
be withdrawn when necessary to protect the project.

## Independence

This is an independently created project. References to an institutional
affiliation identify the author's affiliation and do not represent institutional
sponsorship, approval, or endorsement.
