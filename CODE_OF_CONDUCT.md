# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive
project community. Contributors, users, reviewers, and maintainers are expected
to help create an environment free from harassment and intimidation.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of positive behaviour include:

- communicating respectfully and professionally;
- giving specific, evidence-based technical feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting different levels of experience and background;
- focusing criticism on ideas and implementations, not people;
- protecting confidential and security-sensitive information; and
- accepting maintainer decisions without personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted intimidation;
- insulting, demeaning, sexualised, or inflammatory comments;
- deliberate disruption of discussions or review;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- misrepresenting authorship, affiliation, endorsement, or research results;
- pressuring others to disclose confidential information; and
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  research or software community.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where a person is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the maintainer through a private contact method
listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private method is displayed,
open a neutral issue titled `Private conduct report request` without including
sensitive details; the maintainer will arrange a private channel.

Reports will be considered in good faith and handled as confidentially as
reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Depending on severity and context, the maintainer may:

1. provide a private or public correction;
2. issue a warning;
3. remove comments or contributions;
4. temporarily restrict participation; or
5. permanently ban participation.

Retaliation against a person making a good-faith report is prohibited. The
maintainer has final responsibility for interpreting and enforcing this policy
within the official project spaces.
